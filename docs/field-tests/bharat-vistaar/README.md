# Field test: asking Bharat-VISTAAR to sell or hold

Bharat-VISTAAR is the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare's AI advisory service for farmers, launched in February 2026. Its voice assistant, "Bharati", answers calls on the toll-free helpline 155261 and is meant to cover weather, market prices and crop advice ([source](https://www.insightsonindia.com/2026/02/17/bharat-vistaar/)).

A member of our team called the helpline three times and asked, in Marathi, the question Mandi-Mausam is built to answer: where and when should I sell? The files here are automatic transcriptions of the call audio, kept exactly as they came out. They contain speech-recognition errors, such as misheard crop and place names, and the caller names are the ones our tester used on the calls.

Then said today's banana price at Jalgaon was not available. | No |
| [3](call-3-solapur-jowar-rice.md) | 8 quintals of jowar and 6 of rice near Solapur: which nearby mandi pays well, and when to sell? | Asked whether today's price or a set date was wanted, then said today's jowar and rice prices at Solapur were not available. | No |

## What we took from it

- The assistant was polite and spoke Marathi well, but none of the three calls ended with a price, a comparison between mandis, or a sell-or-hold answer.
- When the mandi asked about had no report that day, it asked the farmer to pick another mandi or date, one at a time, instead of finding the nearest mandi that did report.
- The rain question in call 2 got general advice and an offer to check the forecast, not an answer tied to that farmer's stored crop.

Mandi-Mausam is built for exactly this gap. When the nearest mandis are quiet, it widens the search to those that did report, shows how old each price is and how far away each mandi is, adds the week's storage risk from the weather, and turns it into one answer. Today it is built and tested for onion, so we have not run these banana, jowar and rice questions through it.

Three calls are not a benchmark. We share them as first-hand observations, not a measured comparison.
